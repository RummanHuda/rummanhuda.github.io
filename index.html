Adaptive Noise Cancellation of Speech Using LMS and NLMS Algorithms

  • Applied LMS and NLMS adaptive filters to remove additive noise from speech, using a reference noise signal fed to an adaptive FIR filter.
  • NLMS outperformed LMS on noise suppression: maximum output SNR of 17.58 dB versus 15.05 dB.
